TMRW — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

TMRW is a day planner for tasks, events and habits. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and how to delete it.

Your account

You sign in with Apple. We receive a user identifier from Apple and, unless you choose to hide it, your email address. If you hide it, we only see Apple's private relay address. We use these only to keep your account and sync your plans between your devices.

What we store

  • Your plans (Supabase): the tasks, events, habits, completions and notes you create, and the places you save for location reminders (a name and the place's coordinates). They're stored in our database, hosted by Supabase, so they sync to every device you sign in on. Only your account can read them.
  • Subscriptions (RevenueCat): purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat receives your account identifier and Apple's purchase records to check whether your subscription is active. We never see your payment details.
  • On your phone: settings and a local copy of your plans so the app works offline.

Location

If you add a location reminder, TMRW asks for location access so iOS can notify you when you arrive at or leave a place you saved. Your current location is checked by iOS on your device and never sent to us. Only the places you save are synced.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. We don't use push notification servers or send marketing messages.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale or sharing of your data.
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account, including after a subscription ends, so it's still there if you come back. Delete your account and it's gone.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Delete account in the app. This permanently deletes your account and every task, event, habit and place stored with it straight away. Cancel your subscription separately in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Children

TMRW is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and the new version will be published at this address.
