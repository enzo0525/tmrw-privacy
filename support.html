TMRW — Support

Plan every day with TMRW.

Adding things

Tap + to add a task. Give it a title, then set a date, a time and length, a repeat, or a place with the chips below it. Tasks without a time go in the drawer at the bottom of Today; drag them into your day when you're ready.

Location reminders don't fire

In iPhone Settings → TMRW → Location, choose Always and turn on Precise Location. iOS needs both to notify you when you arrive at or leave a place.

My plans aren't on my other device

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account on both, and that each has a connection. Changes sync when the app opens.

Subscription

Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. On a new phone, tap Restore on the subscription screen. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account removes your account and all your data. See the privacy policy.
